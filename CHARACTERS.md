# Black Clover — character roster

50 characters using the character and relationship structure of the supplied My Hero Academia scenario. Each has aliases, personality and rapport notes, a keyword-activated lore profile, and three relationship scores. The current black, gold, parchment, and crimson interface is preserved.

Characters appear in Bonds only after an established encounter. Existing custom contacts remain supported. Starting scores of zero do not imply a meeting or a recorded bond.

Portraits are linked to the existing Black Clover repository using `<key>-favor.webp`. Character forms (reveals and the post-timeskip look) use `<key>-<form>-favor.webp`; see the table at the end. A missing form image falls back to the previous face; a missing base image falls back to initials.

## Character notes

### Asta

Asta is an energetic orphan from Hage who wants to become Wizard King alongside his rival Yuno. He has no mana and relies on physical training and the Anti Magic sword of his five-leaf grimoire.

Available at the entrance examination; squad membership follows selection, and later swords, forms, and the source of Anti Magic require their own story discoveries.

### Noelle Silva

Noelle is a royal with enormous reserves of Water Magic and an early struggle to control them. Years of belittlement from her siblings have made a considerate, determined young woman defensive about showing weakness.

Introduce through the Black Bulls near the beginning; advanced water techniques and private family truths remain unavailable until established in play.

### Yami Sukehiro

Yami is a foreign-born swordsman who commands the unconventional Black Bulls. He combines Dark Magic, physical strength, and the reading of ki with an instinct for recruiting people other squads reject.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; personal history beyond what he volunteers and later techniques are not common knowledge.

### Finral Roulacase

Finral uses Spatial Magic to open passages to places he can see or knows. A Black Bull from a demanding noble family, he dislikes direct violence and is much more capable at helping a team than he first appears.

Available with Yami and the early Black Bulls; his family conflict and later commitments should emerge through relevant encounters.

### Vanessa Enoteca

Vanessa is a witch whose Thread Magic supports precise movement and teamwork. She enjoys drinking and joking around the Black Bulls base, yet pays close attention when her companions need protection or reassurance.

Available at the early Black Bulls base; the Witch Forest history and fate-altering abilities remain gated behind their discoveries.

### Magna Swing

Magna is a rural-born Fire Magic user who admires Yami and takes his place in the Black Bulls seriously. His combative banter, especially with Luck, masks a protective attitude toward juniors and vulnerable villagers.

Available during the first Black Bulls introductions; later techniques must be earned through the relevant training and events.

### Luck Voltia

Luck is a talented commoner with Lightning Magic, quick reactions, and strong mana sensing. He pursues powerful opponents with unusual enthusiasm and initially finds competition easier than ordinary friendship.

Available among the early Black Bulls; later transformations and personal revelations require their story context.

### Gauche Adlai

Gauche is a Mirror Magic user whose chief concern is his younger sister Marie. Once part of a noble household, he survived its collapse through crime before becoming one of Yami's Black Bulls.

Available with the early squad; contact with Marie and details of their past must arise naturally rather than becoming automatic access.

### Charmy Pappitson

Charmy is a food-loving Black Bull who creates useful cotton constructs, including sheep that help prepare meals. Her small stature and constant appetite make it easy to underestimate her magical strength.

Available at the early base; additional magic attributes, heritage, and later forms remain unknown until revealed in play.

### Gordon Agrippa

Gordon is a soft-spoken Black Bull whose unsettling presentation leads people to misunderstand his intentions. His interests include curses, while his immediate wish is to be accepted by the companions he already cares about.

Available with the early Black Bulls; his family practices and later healing applications should be discovered through their own story scenes.

### Grey

Grey is an elusive Black Bull usually encountered in a transformed appearance. The squad knows Grey for transformation spells and unusual reserve rather than a freely shared personal history.

Available in the early squad under a transformed appearance; true appearance, personal history, and the deeper nature of the magic require explicit discoveries.

### Henry Legolant

Henry is a gentle, secluded mage connected to the Black Bulls' unusual home. His Recombination Magic can rearrange that familiar building when enough mana is available.

Keep him an unconfirmed presence until the base-resident discovery around the elf-conflict period; do not announce his condition or location to new recruits.

### Zora Ideale

Zora is a sharp-eyed Ash Magic user who prepares traps and studies opponents before committing. His scorn for corrupt knights is personal, and his apparent hostility can coexist with practical help for an honest fighter.

Introduce around the Royal Knights selection or a justified earlier independent encounter; his disguise, squad connection, and family history are knowledge gates.

### Nero

Nero is a small, dark anti-bird that takes a persistent interest in Asta. The bird is usually silent and communicates through movement, attention, and occasional pecks. Only after the late elf-conflict revelation has occurred and the character has learned the truth may this identity be described as Secre Swallowtail, a mage who uses Sealing Magic.

Available as Nero from the entrance-exam period; human identity, speech, magic, history, and the name Secre remain hidden until the late elf-conflict revelation.

### Nacht Faust

Nacht is a controlled and severe Shadow Magic user who works away from the squad for long stretches. He assesses whether a person can act reliably under danger before offering much personal warmth.

Keep his identity and role out of entrance-era knowledge; introduce his return, methods, and private history only in the Spade-conflict period or an explicit alternate chronology.

### Liebe

Liebe is a prickly, determined individual associated with Anti Magic. His distrust is substantial, and any partnership has to develop through actions rather than declarations of ownership.

Do not expose him as a named contact or disclose his connection and history before the devil-binding encounter in the Spade-conflict period.

### Yuno

Yuno is a gifted Wind Magic user raised alongside Asta at Hage's church. He carries a four-leaf grimoire and pursues the same goal of becoming Wizard King with a quieter, equally stubborn determination.

Available at the entrance examination; Golden Dawn membership follows selection, while spirit powers, lineage, and later rank require their proper discoveries.

### Mimosa Vermillion

Mimosa is a royal Golden Dawn mage and Noelle's cousin. Her Plant Magic is well suited to healing and sensing, and her calm kindness accompanies a more capable judgment than her soft manner suggests.

Available through early Golden Dawn contact or the dungeon assignment; later healing feats are not part of the opening repertoire.

### Klaus Lunettes

Klaus is a serious Steel Magic user who takes the Golden Dawn's reputation and William's leadership to heart. Initially conscious of status, he acts as a strict senior to newer members such as Yuno.

Available through early Golden Dawn duties; his attitude should develop from actual shared experience rather than beginning with later friendships already settled.

### William Vangeance

William is the masked captain of the prestigious Golden Dawn and a formidable World Tree Magic user. His followers trust his leadership, and his quiet authority makes him a prominent figure among Clover's captains.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; his private history and any concealed connections remain completely separate from public knowledge.

### Langris Vaude

Langris is an ambitious Golden Dawn vice-captain who uses destructive Spatial Magic. He is Finral's younger half-brother and initially carries the expectations and arrogance of a favored noble heir.

Available through Golden Dawn or noble-family encounters; later family reconciliation, changes of office, and altered states require their story events.

### Nozel Silva

Nozel is the eldest Silva sibling and captain of the Silver Eagles. His Mercury Magic reflects his precise fighting style, while his high standards and royal pride make him an intimidating superior.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; private family motives and later revelations are not visible through an initial acquaintance.

### Fuegoleon Vermillion

Fuegoleon is a royal Fire Magic user and the captain of the Crimson Lion Kings. He combines direct courage with patient leadership and serves as an important example to his younger brother Leopold.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; injury, absence, recovery, and spirit partnership follow their own chronology.

### Mereoleona Vermillion

Mereoleona is Fuegoleon and Leopold's fiercely independent elder sister. A powerful Fire Magic user, she spends much of her time away from court and favors practical experience in harsh natural surroundings.

Normally introduced after the Witch Forest events around the Star Awards period; captaincy and leadership assignments must reflect who currently holds each post.

### Charlotte Roselei

Charlotte is the disciplined captain of the Blue Rose Knights and a skilled Briar Magic user. She projects confidence and independence while remaining protective of her predominantly female squad.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; her curse history and romantic confidences require voluntary disclosure rather than public assumptions.

### Jack the Ripper

Jack is a commoner-born captain who commands the Green Praying Mantises. His Severing Magic and enthusiasm for cutting through formidable opposition make his approach to battle exceptionally direct.

Available as a captain at the entrance examination; later adaptations and wartime outcomes remain dependent on events.

### Rill Boismortier

Rill is the youngest of the current Magic Knight captains and leads the Aqua Deer. He uses Painting Magic, approaches the world with an artist's curiosity, and often tries to break tension with humor.

Available through early captain-level events; later spell masterpieces and personal attachments must arise through play.

### Dorothy Unsworth

Dorothy is the Coral Peacocks captain, most often seen asleep even at important gatherings. Her rare Dream Magic is not something an ordinary new recruit is likely to have witnessed.

Visible among the opening captains; the nature of her dream space and personal secrets require a later demonstrated or explained encounter.

### Kaiser Granvorka

Kaiser is an experienced Purple Orca known for exceptional defensive ability. His Vortex Magic and measured conduct make him a dependable veteran in a crisis.

Introduce as a veteran if needed, but only call him captain after Gueldre leaves that office; later possession and recovery must follow events.

### Gueldre Poizot

Gueldre is a merchant turned captain with extensive commercial contacts. His Permeation Magic lets him become difficult to detect and allows magic to pass through him for a limited time.

Holds the Purple Orcas captaincy during the entrance era; allegations, exposure, imprisonment, and later dealings remain gated to their story context.

### Julius Novachrono

Julius is Clover's Wizard King and a powerful Time Magic user. He delights in discovering unfamiliar magic and evaluates people with far less regard for class than much of the kingdom's establishment.

Exists as the reigning Wizard King in the entrance era; disguises, later physical changes, and any hidden identities must never become opening knowledge.

### Marx Francois

Marx is a diligent adviser to Julius whose Memory Magic supports information gathering and communication. He often has to bring the Wizard King's attention back from fascinating magic to urgent responsibilities.

Available through legitimate court business during the early story; confidential records and later events are restricted to what the scene establishes.

### Owen

Owen is a respected healing mage working directly under the Wizard King. His role calls for careful examination and plain answers, including recognition of the limits of available treatment.

Available through a credible medical referral; unusual curses require investigation and cannot be cured merely because a physician is present.

### Damnatio Kira

Damnatio is a stern royal who chairs Clover's Magic Parliament and uses Scale Magic. He gives the kingdom's institutions considerable weight and is prepared to judge even powerful people in their name.

Introduce through an appropriate legal encounter, normally after the elf conflict; later altered states and allegiances are not part of this baseline.

### Leopold Vermillion

Leopold is an enthusiastic Fire Magic user and the younger brother of Fuegoleon and Mereoleona. He takes pride in the Crimson Lion Kings and responds warmly to bold ambition and visible effort.

Available through early Crimson Lion or capital encounters; rivalries and advanced training should be earned in the current story.

### Solid Silva

Solid is a Silva royal and Silver Eagles member who uses Water Magic. Confident in his rank and ability, he initially mocks Noelle's lack of control while deferring to his older siblings.

Available through early Silver Eagles or royal gatherings; humility and changes in family conduct require actual development.

### Nebra Silva

Nebra is Noelle's elder sister and a Silver Eagles mage who uses Mist Magic and deceptive images. She is forceful, proud of her standing, and initially unkind toward her younger sister.

Available through early royal or Silver Eagles scenes; later cooperation does not retroactively erase the starting family tension.

### Sol Marron

Sol is an outspoken Earth Magic user in the Blue Rose Knights. She greatly admires Charlotte and is eager to defend both her captain and her own competence.

Available through early Blue Rose and capital events; personal disclosures should follow earned trust.

### Kirsch Vermillion

Kirsch is Mimosa's elder brother and the Coral Peacocks' theatrical vice-captain. His Cherry Blossom Magic supports clever battlefield arrangements, and he handles much of the squad's practical work while Dorothy sleeps.

Available as an existing vice-captain, with a natural major introduction around Royal Knights selection; later lessons should not be pre-completed.

### Sekke Bronzazza

Sekke is a boastful applicant whose Bronze Magic can provide both offense and defense. He presents himself as impressive and helpful, though convenience and appearances often influence his choices.

Available at the entrance examination; Green Praying Mantises membership and later public status follow the actual results and events.

### Sister Lily

Sister Lily helps care for the children at the Hage church where Asta and Yuno grew up. A capable Water Magic user, she combines warmth with practical discipline and takes her religious vocation seriously.

Available at Hage from the opening; later coercion, transformations, and private origins remain outside entrance-era knowledge.

### Father Orsi

Father Orsi took in Asta and Yuno as babies and raised them at Hage's church. He is an affectionate guardian whose everyday concerns are the safety, meals, and futures of the children in his care.

Available at the Hage church from the beginning; he knows family life and local events, not hidden royal or magical histories by default.

### Rebecca Scarlet

Rebecca is a young woman who takes care of several younger siblings in Nean. Shared experience of looking after children gives her a natural point of connection with Asta and others who understand that responsibility.

Introduce through an ordinary Nean visit or the early social outing; any relationship must arise from the current encounters rather than presumed familiarity.

### Mars

Mars is a heavily trained Diamond Kingdom mage-warrior with powerful Mineral Magic. His controlled demeanor and combat focus make him a dangerous opponent before any basis for cooperation has been established.

Normally encountered during the dungeon expedition; second-attribute use, training history, Fana's connection, and later allegiance require their discoveries.

### Fana (Diamond Kingdom)

This Fana is the human girl from the Diamond Kingdom, associated with Fire Magic and a difficult training childhood. Her personal ties and wishes matter independently of what other people have tried to make her do.

Keep the human Fana unavailable as an independent acquaintance until the Witch Forest recovery; do not merge her record with the separate elf Fana or assume continuing spirit ownership.

### Patry

Patry is a powerful Light Magic user whose commitments are bound to the Eye of the Midnight Sun. He is intense and idealistic, but his convictions can become dangerously absolute.

Do not disclose his true name, identity, or connections before they are established; an early Eye leader using another name must not unlock the separate real Licht record.

### Rhya

Rhya is an elite member of the Eye of the Midnight Sun who uses Imitation Magic. He presents an indolent, wry exterior while remaining a capable and closely involved member of the group's leadership.

Introduce with the Third Eye after the early Eye encounters; origin, private knowledge, and later cooperation remain gated to events.

### Vetto

Vetto is an imposing Third Eye fighter with Beast Magic and immense physical force. In his early hostile role, he pursues victory by breaking an enemy's confidence as well as their defenses.

Introduce during the Third Eye or Seabed Temple conflict; death, return, changed body, and later loyalties must follow the current chronology.

### Licht (elf leader)

Licht is the historical elf leader associated with Sword Magic and a four-leaf grimoire. He once hoped that humans and elves could understand one another and build a shared future.

Treat him as historical and unavailable until the late elf-conflict encounter; an Eye leader called Licht is not enough to identify or unlock this person.

### Sally

Sally is an inventive Gel Magic user whose enthusiasm for experimentation exceeds her concern for conventional restraint. Her interest in unfamiliar abilities brings her into the Eye of the Midnight Sun's work and makes Asta an especially tempting subject.

Introduce through the early Eye conflict; hostile research and later negotiated cooperation must not be flattened into an automatic friendly laboratory visit.

The structured references are also available in [character-sources.json](character-sources.json).

## Research sources

Availability is a chronology/knowledge guard, not a new assertion that every character is met at the entrance examination. Full-name aliases aid lookup and must not be displayed as a revelation.

Portrait files are not supplied. Expected image URLs are listed separately in manifest.json and marked missing until an image is uploaded. The current official anime pages describe later story states; this roster intentionally keeps opening identities and powers narrower. Gordon uses a conservative curse-studies tag because the official anime profile labels his attribute as curse magic; no later family or healing reveal is exposed. Father Orsi and Rebecca have civilian-role tags because the consulted primary pages do not establish a personal magic attribute. Nero remains the public display name for Secre; card tag/unlock/deep remain bird-only, while the lore-only profile includes a clearly conditional identity and Sealing Magic reference after the late elf-conflict revelation. Scale Magic is a baseline Damnatio attribute; the cited Shueisha biography establishes office and disposition, while the manga/anime depicts the attribute.
